## 2. Core Promise / 核心承诺

### EN

Show an overseas buyer how Damon verifies a supplier's manufacturing claim in the real world. The point is not to say "factory good, trading company bad." The point is to establish who actually makes the product and whether the production story is consistent from product → line → capability → finished goods.

### CN

让海外买家看到 Damon 不是听供应商说“我们是工厂”就结束，而是进入现场核实：这个产品到底是不是这里生产、对应哪条线、工厂真正做什么、成品和包装能不能与生产现场对应起来。

本片不是“工厂一定比贸易公司好”，而是强调：**采购前先搞清楚谁真正生产、谁承担什么角色。**

### B2B Safety Filter

- 不把 `factory` 自动等于“好供应商”。
- 不把 `trading company` 自动等于“坏供应商”。
- 不制造抓包 / 揭黑 / 骗局叙事。

- 不公开客户箱唛、订单号、联系人、内部单据或其他敏感信息。

### Accuracy Boundary

- Line 2 does **not** say a showroom / warehouse is suspicious; only that neither alone proves manufacturing.
- Line 3 must show the same product or a clearly verified matching product. If the exact red-and-white bowl is not running that day, change the line to: `Then I check which line actually makes this product.`
- Line 4 is a general sourcing principle; do **not** imply Wanhui sources products elsewhere unless factory staff explicitly says so. The visual should be Damon asking about capability boundaries, not accusing.
- Line 5 uses finished goods as consistency evidence, not as standalone proof of factory status.

## 6. Spoken Version / Damon 现场口语版

现场真人优先记这 6 个短句，不要求背长稿：

1. `They say they make this. I want to see where.`
2. `First — show me the line.`
3. `Same product. Same line. That's what I want to check.`
4. 对工厂人员中文问：`这款是在这条线做的吗？这条线主要做哪些产品？`
5. `A trading company isn't automatically bad. I just need to know who makes the product.`
6. `Finding is step one. Verifying is the real work.`

### 核心编导原则

- 不从厂门牌匾开场；“招牌写着工厂”本身不是本片的证据。
- 生产线镜头必须服务“核实”，不要重拍 V006 的完整工艺教程。
- V006 是 `How is it made?`；V008 是 `Who actually makes it, and how do I verify that?`
- 仓库只作为一致性证据，不拍成 V005 的库存 / 价格内容。
- 如果当天生产线没有跑这款产品，就诚实说 / 剪成“确认对应生产线”，不要假装正在生产同款。

## 13. Content Memory / 内容记忆

### Main Question

`When a supplier says they're a factory, how does Damon verify who actually makes the product?`

### Core Buyer Value

- 看生产线不等于完整核实；要把具体产品与生产能力对应起来。
- 明确工厂能力边界，比简单问“你是不是工厂”更有价值。

- Damon gives sourcing judgment

## 14. Do Not Repeat

后续不要再做一条“进工厂看到机器 = 证明是工厂”的同质内容。

后续 supplier-verification 应进入新问题：

- 如何核实某个具体产品是不是自产
- 怎么确认工厂有没有你需要的 customization 能力
- 如何问产能 / 交期而不只听口头承诺
- 样品、量产、出货前怎么做一致性检查
- 工厂和贸易公司的角色什么时候各有优势
