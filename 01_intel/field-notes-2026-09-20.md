## 2. Public-source verification notes

### IMC 临沂国际贸易中心

- Official wording found in Linyi government sources: **IMC 临沂国际贸易中心**.
- Public positioning: international-trade service / enterprise cluster, international selection center, foreign-trade ecosystem and one-stop services.
- 2026 public reporting states phase I had attracted 98 signed enterprises and includes an international selection center and one-window government service functions.
- Content angle: `Linyi is not only traditional wholesale markets — it is also building dedicated foreign-trade infrastructure.`

Sources:
- 临沂市人民政府，《IMC打造商城国际贸易总部集聚区》，2024-03-15: https://www.linyi.gov.cn/info/9321/372214.htm
